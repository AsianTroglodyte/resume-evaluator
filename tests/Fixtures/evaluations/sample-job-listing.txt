Junior Data Operations Assistant — Northstar Health Network

Location: Stamford, CT (three days on site)
Employment type: Full-time, entry level

About the role
Northstar Health Network is looking for a junior data operations assistant to support
our clinical reporting team. You will help maintain internal data tools, respond to
data-quality requests, and work with analysts and operations staff.

Responsibilities
- Update and troubleshoot internal reporting tools using SQL and Python scripts
- Review incoming data files, investigate routine errors, and document recurring issues
- Build simple dashboards and spreadsheet reports for operations teams
- Participate in ticket triage and communicate progress to non-technical colleagues

Requirements
- Associate's or bachelor's degree in information systems, computer science, data analytics, or related field
- Familiarity with SQL and spreadsheets; basic Python is preferred
- Ability to organize work, explain issues clearly, and collaborate with a team
- Interest in data quality, reporting, or healthcare operations

Nice to have
- Experience with Power BI, Tableau, or another dashboard tool
- Knowledge of Git, Linux, REST APIs, or automated testing
- Previous help desk, customer service, or internship experience
